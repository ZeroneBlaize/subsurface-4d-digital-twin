End-to-End Computational Workflow

From raw exploratory borehole drilling chatter and split-spoon sampler friction to continuous 2.5D Digital Elevation Model normalization, automated IS:6403 bearing capacity, and 4D IoT piezometer voxelization.

Lead Investigator: Sahil Deshpande · School of Earth, Ocean & Climate Sciences / School of Infrastructure, IIT Bhubaneswar
01
Data Ingestion & IS:1498 Classification

Borehole Parsing & Lithological Normalization

Raw drilling logs arrive with depths recorded relative to arbitrary driller collar grades. Split-spoon SPT N-values and laboratory index tests (liquid limit, plasticity index) are classified per IS:1498 into standardized groups (CH, CI, CL, SM, Basalt, Gneiss).

MODULE: src.borehole_processor IS:1498-1970
Input: Raw CSV / Excel Drilling Logs
Columns: [BH_ID, Collar_Depth, Stratum, Field_N, LL, PI]
Output: Normalized Stratigraphic Contact Intervals
02
Geospatial Terrain Coupling

Topographic Reduced Level (R.L.) Normalization

Bilinear interpolation across surrounding DEM raster cells assigns continuous ground surface elevations $Z_{\text{surf}}(x, y)$. Driller depths convert into absolute geodetic Reduced Levels: $Z_{\text{bot}, k} = Z_{\text{surf}} - d_k$.

MODULE: src.dem_handler EPSG:32644 (UTM)
$$Z_{\text{surf}}(x, y) = (1-u)(1-v)Z_{i,j} + u(1-v)Z_{i+1,j} + \dots$$ $$Z_{\text{wt}}(x, y) = Z_{\text{surf}}(x, y) - D_w$$
03
Geometric Physics Guard

Downward Monotonic Stacking Guard

Standard thin-plate spline or kriging interpolations across undulating terrain routinely invert or cross strata boundaries. The monotonic stacking engine enforces non-intersection, allowing natural layer pinch-outs without negative thicknesses ($T_k \ge 0$).

MODULE: src.spatial_interpolator SUPERPOSITION LAW
$$Z_0(x, y) \equiv Z_{\text{surf}}(x, y)$$ $$Z_k(x, y) = \min\left(Z_{k-1}(x, y), \, \hat{Z}_k(x, y)\right)$$ $$T_k(x, y) = Z_{k-1}(x, y) - Z_k(x, y) \ge 0.0\text{ m}$$
04
Automated Indian Standard Geotechnics

IS:2131 SPT Corrections & IS:6403 Continuous SBC

Applies Peck-Hanson-Thornburn overburden confinement $C_N = \sqrt{100/\sigma'_{v0}}$ and hydrodynamic dilatancy corrections $N'$ when submerged. Computes Vesic general/local shear failure ($\phi < 28^\circ$, Cl. 5.2) with continuous water factor $W'$ and Teng-Bowles settlement envelope ($S_a \le 25\text{ mm}$).

MODULE: src.geotech_engine IS:6403 & IS:2131
$$q_{nu} = c N_c s_c d_c + q(N_q - 1)s_q d_q W' + \frac{1}{2}\gamma B N_\gamma s_\gamma d_\gamma W'$$ $$\mathbf{SBC}(x, y) = \min\left(q_{ns} + \gamma D_f, \, q_{\text{settlement}}\right)$$
05
Stochastic Heterogeneity

Coupled Markov Chains & Shannon Entropy

Transitions across horizontal and vertical persistence ratios ($K = l_h / l_v$) generate 50 stochastic lithological realizations. Shannon Information Entropy $H(x, z) = -\sum p_k \log_2 p_k$ pinpoints unmapped sand lenses and optimal exploration coordinates.

MODULE: src.generative_subsurface Elfeki & Dekking (2001)
$$P(S_{i,j}=k \mid S_{i-1,j}=l, S_{i,j-1}=m) = \frac{P_{lk}^h \cdot P_{mk}^v}{\sum P_{lr}^h \cdot P_{mr}^v}$$ Peak Entropy Anomaly: $H = 1.78\text{ bits}$ between BH-04 and BH-05.
06
4D Cyber-Physical Ingestion

3D Multi-Physics Voxelization & IoT Telemetry

Hexahedral 3D voxel grid assimilates live vibrating-wire piezometer telemetry ($PZ\text{-}01, PZ\text{-}02$). Dynamically updates hydrostatic pore water pressure $u(t)$, Terzaghi effective stress $\sigma'_v$, and Janbu non-linear operational stiffness $E'(\sigma'_v)$ in 2.42 seconds.

MODULE: src.digital_twin_engine Janbu (1963) Power Law
$$\sigma'_v(x, y, z, t) = \max\left(1.0, \, \sigma_v - u(x, y, z, t)\right)$$ $$E'(x, y, z, t) = E_0 \left(\frac{\sigma'_v(x, y, z, t)}{p_{\text{atm}}}\right)^m$$
07
Vendor-Neutral Dissemination

DuckDB Spatial Catalog, Pure-Python VTK & GeoBIM

Zero-copy DuckDB OLAP engine indexes 3,800+ Indian borehole records for sub-millisecond queries ($<0.8\text{ ms}$). Exports binary-encoded pure-Python VTK XML ImageData (`.vti`) for ParaView, OpenGeoSys FEA, and QGIS GeoPackages (`.gpkg`).

MODULES: src.spatial_database, src.exporter buildingSMART IFC 4.3
DuckDB: `data/db/subsurface_geotech.duckdb`
Formats: Pure XML VTK (`.vti`), GeoTIFF, GeoPackage (`.gpkg`)
Interoperability: QGIS, ParaView, OpenGeoSys, IFC 4.3 `IfcGeotechnicalStratum`
